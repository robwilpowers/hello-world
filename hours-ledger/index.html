Weeks run Monday to Sunday and carry ISO week numbers. A week that spans New Year counts toward the year that holds its Thursday; the time-entries export lists every block by its real date if you need calendar-year totals. Weeks before your first logged week are left out; after that, a week with nothing logged counts as zero hours. Each week keeps the targets that applied when you logged it, so changing a target later does not rewrite past weeks.
